## Welcome

This course is about doing, not reading. It takes 4 weeks (one month), with a short task that takes about 5 minutes a day. You do it, then you write it down.

**The rules**

1. Do the task every day, even on bad days. 5 minutes counts.
2. Write it down straight away. If it's not written, it didn't happen.
3. Aim for done, not perfect.
4. Bring your log to your next session.

**What you need:** a notebook (or notes app), a calendar, and a timer.

**Missed a day?** Just do today's. Don't try to catch up.

<Aside type="note" title="Your log lives in this browser">
	You can fill in the log tables right on each week's page. Everything you type is saved only in this browser on this device, so keep using the same device and browser. Clearing your browsing data will erase it.
</Aside>
