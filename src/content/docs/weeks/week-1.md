**Goal:** See what really happens in your day, then get everything onto one list.

**Days 1 to 3, every evening (5 min)**

1. Write the 3 things you planned to do today.
2. Mark each one: done or not done.
3. For anything not done, write what you did instead.

**Day 4 setup (10 min):** Pick one calendar and one task list. Put every appointment and task you can think of into them.

**Days 4 to 7, every morning (5 min)**

1. Mark each task **A** (today), **B** (soon) or **C** (can wait).
2. Start your top A task now, for at least 2 minutes.
3. In the evening, tick off what you finished.
