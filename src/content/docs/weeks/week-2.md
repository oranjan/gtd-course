**Goal:** Turn tasks you avoid into small steps, then start them even when you don't feel like it.

**Every day (5 min)**

1. Pick one task you keep putting off.
2. Write it as 3 to 5 steps of 15 minutes or less. ("Do my taxes" becomes: find last year's form, collect receipts, fill in page 1, and so on.)
3. Set a timer for 5 minutes and do step 1. You can stop when it rings.
4. Write how long you actually worked, and give yourself a small reward.

**Bonus rule:** If something takes 2 minutes or less, do it now.
