**Goal:** Handle distractions and the thoughts that stop you.

**Days 15 to 17, every day (5 min or more)**

1. Put your phone in another room or face down, and a "later" note next to you.
2. Start a task and a timer. Stop the timer when your mind wanders. Write the time.
3. Every time you want to check something, write it on the "later" note instead.

**Days 18 to 21, every time you feel stuck (5 min)**

1. Write the thought exactly as it came.
2. Write a more helpful version.
3. Do one small action straight away.

**Examples:** "This will only take 5 minutes" → "Let me time it." "If it's not perfect, why bother?" → "Done is better than perfect." "I always mess up" → "What's one thing I can do now?"
